FAQ

Good to know

Is anything booked when I press "Send on WhatsApp"?

No. Your request is saved and opens in WhatsApp for you to send. Nothing is booked or charged until our team confirms availability and price and you say yes.

What does "best price guaranteed" mean?

We work directly with hotels, drivers and operators. If you show us a genuine, like-for-like lower quote, we'll match it or beat it.

Which vehicles can I book?

Hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, Innova and Crysta, Tempo Travellers (12 and 17 seater), Urbania, mini buses and full Volvo coaches. We also help with Volvo and HRTC bus seats.

Do you cover all of Himachal?

Yes, from Shimla to Spiti and Dalhousie to Kinnaur. Our office is in Kullu, and our partners cover the whole state.

How do I pay?

Payment details come with your confirmation, directly from our team on WhatsApp. We never ask for card details on this website.
